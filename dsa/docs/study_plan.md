## Tips

1. Study templates and understand patterns before grinding problems.
2. For each problem: try brute force first, then optimize.
3. If stuck for 20+ minutes, look at hints, then try again.
4. After solving, always write comments explaining approach + complexity.
5. Revisit problems you struggled with after 3-7 days (spaced repetition).
